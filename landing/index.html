01

nothing leaves without your yes — verifiably.

every outward action stops at an approval card: web, menubar, or buttons on your phone. the state machine is race-proof, edited drafts run exactly once, and every departure is written to a hash-chained ledger you can re-verify with one click. other agents ask you to trust them. ro hands you the math.

02

it lives where your life actually is.

text your own number and ro answers. it reads your mail, knows your calendar, remembers every message you've ever sent — a lifetime archive in your own postgres, no retention limits, no vendor terms. cloud agents get an oauth slice of you; ro gets the whole picture, because the whole picture never leaves the machine.

03

vault lanes: some things never touch a cloud.

tag a contact, a channel, or your finance agent as vault and it is only ever processed by local models — the taint follows the data into memory, so it can never leak into a cloud-bound prompt. flip airgap and the whole agent runs offline. minimization by architecture, not by policy.

04

teach it once, own it forever.

describe a task in plain words and ro writes the playbook. hire named bots with charters that delegate to each other — every handoff logged, no hidden channels. schedule anything, trigger on events, dry-run in shadow mode with guaranteed zero egress before you arm it.

05

it eats both open ecosystems.

drop any of the thousands of mcp servers into one config file and ro gains its tools. point it at any folder of agent skills and ro gains its procedures. all of it arrives pre-wrapped in the approval gate. the subscription agents consume neither.

06

it gets better every week — with proof.

ro studies your edits and rejections, proposes new style rules as approval cards (nothing rewrites its brain without you), and re-runs its eval suite so a personality change can never silently break behavior. at 3:30am it sharpens its own memory on free local compute. you wake up to the changelog.
